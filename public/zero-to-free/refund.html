Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

We want you to be happy with the Kit. If it isn't right for you, here's how refunds work.

7-day refund

  • You can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase.
  • Email us with the email address you paid with and your Razorpay payment ID.
  • No complicated questions. A one-line reason helps us improve, but it isn't required.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method through Razorpay. We start the refund within 2 working days of approval. Your bank or UPI app usually shows it within 5 to 7 working days.

After 7 days

Because the Kit is a digital product delivered instantly, refunds are not available after 7 days from purchase.

Cancellation

The Kit is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel later. If a payment fails or you are charged twice, contact us and we will refund the duplicate or failed charge in full.

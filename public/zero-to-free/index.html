The problem

You were sold one road.

Degree, placement, job. Then forty years of someone else deciding your hours and your ceiling.

Maybe you dropped out. Maybe college taught you nothing useful. Maybe you just know you don't want that road.

You don't need a degree to work in AI. Companies pay for proof. And you don't need to stay in a job forever.

A job is a good dream. You can have it and live a good life. But on its own it isn't freedom. Used right, it's the funding round for your freedom.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or buy the Kit, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Purchase details: your name, email address, phone number and payment status, received from Razorpay when you buy.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.
  • Basic technical data: the website host and font provider may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type.

What we don't collect

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay under their own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To deliver the Kit and send your access link
  • To answer support and refund requests
  • To send important updates about the Kit you bought

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the store: Razorpay (payments), our email provider (sending your access link), and our website host. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as needed for delivery, support, and tax and legal requirements.

The video plays three short, panicked calls from family members and asks which one is a real person. None of them is. All three were made with AI voices in a few seconds.

## How little audio a clone needs

The same kind of tool can copy a real person's voice. In a 2023 test, McAfee researchers got an 85% voice match from just three seconds of audio. That is about one story video. It is one benchmark, not a general rate, but it shows how low the bar is.

## How the scam works

The FBI warns that criminals use AI-generated audio of a loved one in a crisis to ask relatives for money or a ransom. The voice on the phone can sound exactly like you.

## What to do

- **Agree a family safe word.** The FBI advises a secret word or phrase that only your family knows. Keep it off the internet and out of your group chats.
- **If the caller can't say it, hang up.** Then call the person back yourself on a number you know is theirs, as the FTC recommends.

Send this to your family and pick your word tonight.
